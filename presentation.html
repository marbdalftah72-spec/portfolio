16-bit Computer with Hardwired CPU

Designed and simulated a complete general-purpose 16-bit computer processor from fundamental logic gates up to micro-operations using Logisim Evolution.

⚙️ Datapath & Register Units

  • Register Architecture: Engineered AR (12-bit address), PC (Program Counter), IR (Instruction Register), DR (Data Register), AC (Accumulator), INPR, and OUTR.
  • 16-bit ALU Design: Full arithmetic (ADD), logic (AND, Complement), and shifting micro-operations with common bus multiplexing.
  • Processor Status Flags: Implemented E (Extended Carry), Z (Zero Detection), FGI (Input Flag), and FGO (Output Flag).

🧠 Hardwired Control & Execution

  • Control Unit: Sequence counter with 3-to-8 instruction decoding for precise micro-operation timing signals (T0–T6).
  • Instruction Set: Full support for Memory-Reference (AND, ADD, LDA, STA, BUN, BSA, ISZ) and Register/IO instructions.
  • Memory Subsystem: 1024 words × 16 bits organized memory unit with simulated fetch, decode, and execute cycles.
